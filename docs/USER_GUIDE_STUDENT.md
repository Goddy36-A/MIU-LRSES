# Student guide

Status: covers Phases 1-6.

- **Dashboard**: your number of courses this semester and your next lectures.
- **My timetable**: lectures of the courses you are enrolled in, grouped by day. *Upcoming* and *Past* tabs. Cancelled lectures are crossed out and labelled. All times are Africa/Kampala.
- **My courses**: the courses you are enrolled in and who teaches them.
- You can only see your own timetable and courses. Change your password from the menu at the top right ("Change password"). "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page starts a reset.
- If you see "profile has not been set up", ask the administrator.

## Notifications (menu: Notifications)
Lecture **reminders** and **schedule-change notices** (a lecture moved or cancelled) appear here, with an unread count in the top bar. Click a notification to read it; you can mark items as read. Reminders appear only in this system - they are not sent by email, SMS or WhatsApp - and only after the system has processed them, so check the page regularly. If you take part in the research study, whether you receive reminders can depend on your study group; schedule-change notices are always sent.

## My attendance, Assignment deadlines, My results
- **My attendance**: your recorded attendance per course. Lectures where nothing was recorded are shown as "not recorded", not as absences.
- **Assignment deadlines**: upcoming deadlines for your courses.
- **My results**: marks your lecturers have entered. Unmarked work is shown as not marked.
You can only see your own records.

## Questionnaires (menu: Questionnaires)
If a researcher has assigned you a questionnaire, you will see it here with its instructions. Taking part in research is voluntary and depends on the consent you gave the researcher; answer honestly - there are no right or wrong answers on opinion (Likert) questions. Submitted answers can be seen by the research team, not by your lecturers.
