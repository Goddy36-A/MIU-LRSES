# Lecturer guide

Status: covers Phases 1-6 (analytics and exports, Phase 7, are not part of the lecturer role).

- **Dashboard**: number of assigned course offerings and your next lectures.
- **My courses**: courses assigned to you. Open one to see its enrolled students and lectures.
- **My lectures**: lectures of your assigned courses only. Search by course/topic/venue; filter by semester, venue, status and dates.
- **Schedule a lecture**: pick one of your courses, a venue and the start/end time. You are recorded as the lecturer. If there is a clash (you, the venue, the course, or a student who attends both), the form tells you what clashes.
- **Reschedule / Cancel**: open the lecture. A reason is required and is shown in the change history. Cancelled lectures cannot be changed.
- You cannot see or change other lecturers' lectures, manage venues, or open student records outside your course rosters.
- If you see "profile has not been set up", ask the administrator to register you as a lecturer.

## Attendance
Open one of your lectures and record each student as present, absent, late or excused. You can correct a record later; every correction keeps the earlier value and who changed it. A student you have not recorded is shown as "not recorded", **not** as absent. Attendance reports for your courses are available from the same area. Rules: `ATTENDANCE_PERFORMANCE.md`.

## Assignments, assessments and marks (menu: Assignments, Assessments)
Create assignments with instructions and a deadline, record submissions (late ones are flagged against the deadline), create assessments with a maximum mark, and record marks. A mark you have not entered is shown as "not marked", never as zero. Course reports summarise punctuality and performance. You see only courses assigned to you.

## Not part of your role
Reminders are generated for students automatically; you do not manage them. You cannot see research participants or questionnaire data.
